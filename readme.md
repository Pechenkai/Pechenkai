# Vasiliy — Backend & ML Engineer

I build reliable data-driven services and machine-learning systems, with a focus on **search, ranking, and recommendation**.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in **Software Engineering** from **Bauman Moscow State Technical University (BMSTU)** and am currently pursuing a Master's degree in **Data Analytics** at the **Higher School of Economics (HSE)**. I am developing expertise at the intersection of backend engineering, data platforms, and applied ML.

## Focus areas

- **Search & recommender systems** — retrieval, ranking, relevance, and offline evaluation
- **ML engineering** — experimentation, model training, and practical delivery
- **Data engineering** — scalable batch pipelines and workflow orchestration
- **Backend development** — maintainable services and integrations
